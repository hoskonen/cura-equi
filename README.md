# 🐎 Cura Equi — Horse Needs

*A companion should not be merely ridden, but cared for.*

Cura Equi brings life to your horse by introducing natural hunger, feeding, and satisfaction systems that evolve over time.  
Your mount now truly feels like a living creature — a companion whose well-being depends on your care.

---

## ⚒ Cycle of Feeding

Horses grow hungry as time and distance pass.  
Feed them properly to keep them sated and in good condition.  
Different foods have different effects — from humble carrots to full oat or hay bundles.

---

## 🌾 Sated Tiers & Behavior

Feeding provides varying “sated” durations that prevent over-feeding.  
Your horse cannot be endlessly stuffed — you must plan your feedings as days go by.  
Icons and in-game feedback indicate current hunger and sated state.

---

## 💤 Sleep and Wait Integration

Time skips such as sleeping or waiting advance hunger realistically.  
Your horse’s condition catches up with the hours that pass, ensuring consistency with real time flow.

---

## 🐴 Grazing and Natural Recovery

When unmounted and outdoors during daylight, your horse may graze.  
Grazing restores a small amount of satiety, encouraging breaks on long journeys.

---

## 💾 Persistence & Performance

All hunger and sated states are saved and restored seamlessly across sessions.  
Lightweight timers and watchers ensure minimal performance impact, even with long playtimes or multiple horses.

---

## 🔧 Configuration and Compatibility

Cura Equi is designed to integrate smoothly with other immersion-focused mods.  
All core systems (feeding, grazing, persistence) are modular and script-based.  
The mod can safely coexist with most gameplay and UI modifications.

---

## 🧭 Development Notes

- Built for *Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2* using Lua scripting and custom buff logic.  
- Implements timed states, save/load persistence, and multi-horse GUID tracking.  
- Includes robust debug logging for testing hunger, feeding, and sleep catch-up.
